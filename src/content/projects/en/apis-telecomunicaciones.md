## The context

A high-volume telecom operator with a backend already in production, serving three processes: sales, line activations and product management. At that scale the APIs take constant load and data has to stay consistent across systems. Silent failures are not an option.

## What I did

I worked on that existing system. I built new features and changed existing ones, and a good part of the job was optimisation: making the APIs perform better without breaking anything that already worked.
